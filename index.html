About Us

Blue Treasure Association is dedicated to the protection and restoration of marine ecosystems. We work through education, advocacy, research, and collaborative action to ensure a sustainable future for our oceans.

Our Objectives

  • Protect marine biodiversity and preserve ecosystems.
  • Restore coral reefs, mangroves, and seagrass beds.
  • Promote sustainable fishing practices.
  • Support the establishment of Marine Protected Areas.
  • Prevent marine pollution through awareness and action.
  • Promote climate resilience through adaptation and mitigation.
  • Raise public awareness and foster ocean literacy.
  • Advocate for effective marine policies and governance.
  • Conduct scientific research and monitoring programs.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders for collective impact.
